Browsers drop background images when printing unless the reader has ticked "Background graphics", so a background version simply vanishes from the exported PDF. Set `export_photo: false` in _config.yml to drop the photo from the export only; it stays on screen. The two full-bleed rules above and below the contact line are drawn here rather than as borders on the row, because in the reference design they run the whole width of the paper, past the page margins. {%- endcomment -%}
